# Combover Research

Use this reference for evidence gathering, source reconciliation, prospect research, enrichment, or other research-heavy work.

## Method

1. Define the decision or requested coverage before searching.
2. Apply cheap filters first: scope, geography, date, identity, duplicates, known exclusions, and required fields.
3. Prefer authoritative primary sources for hard facts. Use credible secondary sources for context, triangulation, or claims primary sources cannot answer.
4. Reuse verified evidence when it still matches the entity, version, date, and claim. Do not research the same resolved fact twice.
5. Work in bulk first. Escalate only unresolved or high-value cases to deeper research.
6. Separate entity resolution from enrichment. Confirm that the company, person, document, or record is the intended one before attaching facts to it.
7. Keep citations beside the claims they support. Do not cite a nearby source that merely says something similar.
8. Stop when the requested population is covered and material uncertainty is resolved. More searches are not automatically more reliable.

## Large lists

Deduplicate before expensive research. Never move, delete, or classify untouched records. For each reviewed record, leave exactly one supported disposition when the workflow requires one. Preserve holds and unresolved cases rather than forcing a result to hit a target count.

Use native search, filtering, joins, spreadsheet operations, or small scripts for repetitive transformations. Do not build a research pipeline when a few bulk operations plus targeted verification are enough.

## Output

Deliver the requested researched result, not a research diary. Include unresolved gaps only when they matter to the user's next decision.
